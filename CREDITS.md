## Textures

- Wood floor and brick: from the [three.js examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures) (MIT).
- Desk wood: `woodAlbedo.png` from [Babylon.js Assets](https://github.com/BabylonJS/Assets) (CC BY 4.0).
- Plaster, rug, keyboard, posters and neon sign are generated in the browser.
